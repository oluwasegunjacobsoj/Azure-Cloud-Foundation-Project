Step by Step on How I subscribed to my Microsoft Azure

I first signed up using my email address and received a SMS text code

I went to my local bank to issue me a Naira Mastercard so I could pay the $1 dollar equivalent, that was refunded back into my account, it was just for verification. I initially got a Verve card from another bank and I realized that verve card weren't accepted. Just Mastercard, American Express & Visa.

Then I navigate to the top right corner of my portal.azure.com page to locate the DEFAULT DIRECTORY

After clicking on it, it brought two major options, 'My Microsoft account' and 'Switch directory', then I selected 'My Microsoft account'

Then it takes me to the account.microsoft.com - Microsoft account page

There are a series of menu here, I often find the payment options or I often find the option to register for the free tier as either a student or just a free tier or as a pay-as-you-go user. However, on my research I found out that as I am not a learner presently at any higher institution, I do not have any email address attached to a particular higher institution and so, I have to pick the free tier

Also it can be found using the global search method, by going to the top search bar for the Azure portal, I typed subscriptions and clicked on the service. Because my account was empty, Azure displayed a large banner at the top of the blank screen that read: "Don't have a subscription? Get started with an Azure free trial" OR an Add button that routes me to the activation portal.

On the primary landing page, Azure displayed quick start tiles for new users. I clicked the tile labeled " Start with an Azure free trial" or "Claim your $200 free credit"

The free tier has $200 free worth of credit, which is about 300,000 naira worth of free credit. At creation I can find my free credit balance on my Home dashboard. I gives me June 17th, 2026 for my credit to expire.
